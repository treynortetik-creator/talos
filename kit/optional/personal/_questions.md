# Question queue

One at a time, at a natural moment. Mark the date when answered; never re-ask.

⚠️ **Check TOPICAL coverage before asking, not just exact matches.** An unasked question about
something the notes already cover is still a re-ask, and it lands the same way. If coverage
exists, either skip it or ask a genuinely new angle — and enrich the existing note rather than
starting cold.

Add your own as you go — the good ones usually come from something half-said in
passing that deserved a follow-up.
