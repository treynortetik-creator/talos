# Weekly Status Rollup

**Trigger:** Fridays, 3pm.
**Output:** one email, under 200 words, to the team.

## Steps

1. **Gather.** Pull anything that changed this week from `projects/`.
   *What good looks like:* every active project accounted for, including the ones
   with no movement — "no change" is information.
2. **Cut.** Three items max. Rank by what someone would act on.
   *What good looks like:* if a reader would not do anything differently knowing
   it, it does not make the cut.
3. **Write.** One line each: what happened, what it means, what is next.
   *What good looks like:* no adjectives, no "excited to share," a named owner on
   anything outstanding.
4. **Flag risks.** Anything slipping gets said plainly in its own line.
   *What good looks like:* the bad news is in the first half, not buried at the end.

**Draft only. I review and send it myself.**
